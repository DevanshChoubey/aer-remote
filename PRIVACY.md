# Privacy policy

Effective 2026-10-30. Applies to Aer Remote (`com.devanshchoubey.aerremote`) on Android and iOS.

Aer Remote is an unofficial app made by one developer, Devansh Choubey. It is not made by or
affiliated with Godrej.

## The short version

Aer Remote collects nothing and sends nothing. It has no account, no server, no analytics and no
ads, and release builds on Android hold no internet permission.

## What stays on your phone

To work, the app keeps these on your phone only:

- the dispensers you add: their Bluetooth address, the name you give them and their home time zone;
- the last status read from each dispenser (battery, refill counters, firmware) and its schedules;
- your theme and alert settings, and which alerts have already been shown.

None of it leaves the phone. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage, deletes it.

## Permissions

| Permission                               | Why                                                                   |
| ---------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Nearby devices (Bluetooth scan, connect) | To find your dispenser and send it commands. Never used for location. |
| Notifications                            | For refill and battery alerts, which you can turn off in Settings.    |
| Run at startup, vibrate, keep awake      | To keep scheduled alerts after a restart and show them.               |
| Launcher badges (some phone makers)      | Added by the notification library to show a count on the app icon.    |

The app never asks for location on Android 12 or newer, or on iOS.

## Sharing

The app shares no data with anyone, because it collects none. Nothing is sold.

## Children

The app is not aimed at children and collects no data from anyone.
